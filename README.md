# SolveX

### See it. Solve it. Understand it.

SolveX is an AI-powered educational assistant designed to help students understand and solve academic problems.

## What SolveX Does

- 📷 Scan questions from images
- ✍️ Ask questions by typing
- 🧮 Solve calculation-based problems
- 📚 Explain non-calculation questions
- 🧠 Provide step-by-step solutions
- 💬 Answer follow-up questions
- 🎯 Generate practice questions
- 📊 Track learning progress

## Subjects

SolveX is being designed to support:

- Mathematics
- Further Mathematics
- Physics
- Chemistry
- Biology
- Statistics
- Computer Science
- Economics
- Government
- Geography
- English
- And more

## Our Mission

SolveX is built to make learning easier, faster, and more interactive.

Students can use SolveX to understand questions, work through calculations, explore difficult topics, and practice what they learn.

## Built for Students

SolveX is designed around the way students actually learn:

- See a problem
- Understand what it is asking
- Work through the solution
- Understand the reasoning
- Practice similar problems
- Ask follow-up questions
